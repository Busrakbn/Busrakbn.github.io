Cafe Nekozy – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy explains what information the game Cafe Nekozy ("the game", "we") handles when you play it on Android or in a web browser.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information ourselves. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no analytics of its own. We do not ask for your name, e-mail address, contacts, photos or any other personal data, and we do not run servers that receive data from the game.

2. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (days played, coins, purchases in the in-game shop, quests, cat friendship and similar) and your settings (language, music and sound volume) are saved only on your own device. This data is not sent to us. You can delete it at any time with Settings > Reset Save in the game, or by uninstalling the game.

3. Advertising in the Android version

The Android version of the game shows advertisements: optional "watch an ad" rewards (for example extra coins) and an occasional full-screen ad between days. These ads are provided by the Yodo1 MAS advertising service (Yodo1 Ltd), which works with advertising partners such as Google AdMob and other ad networks to choose and show the ads.

To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Yodo1 and its advertising partners may collect and process, according to their own privacy policies:

  • your device's advertising ID (Google Advertising ID) and other device identifiers;
  • device information such as model, operating system, language, screen size and network type;
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it (country or city, not GPS);
  • information about the ads you see and tap.

On first launch the game shows a privacy dialog from Yodo1 that asks for your age and, where the law requires it (for example in the EU, the UK and some US states), for your consent to personalised ads. If you do not consent, or if you are under the age of digital consent, only non-personalised ads are shown. You can also reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalisation at any time in your device's settings (on most Android devices: Settings > Google > Ads or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

More information: Yodo1 Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from apps that use its services

4. Web game portals

When you play Cafe Nekozy on a web game portal (for example CrazyGames or Poki), the portal runs the game inside its own website. The portal may show advertisements and may collect information according to its own privacy policy, which applies to your use of that website. The web version does not include the Yodo1 advertising service.

5. In-app purchases

The game does not sell anything for real money. All shop items are bought with coins earned by playing.

6. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. When the age entered in the privacy dialog is below the age of digital consent, the advertising service is told so and shows only non-personalised, age-appropriate ads.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
